Engineering philosophy

Architecture as informed decisions

Good architecture is not about introducing complexity. It is about making informed decisions that support business needs, maintainability, and sustainable evolution.

Connect decisions to implementation

An architecture is only as good as the code and operations that follow it, so I stay hands-on.

Respect production constraints

Team capacity, existing systems and business continuity shape the right answer as much as technology.

Make trade-offs explicit

Options, consequences and review triggers are written down so teams can revisit them.

Keep learning

Currently deepening platform engineering: internal developer platforms, golden paths and developer experience.

Career

How the work has evolved

From GIS and field applications to leading platform delivery and trading infrastructure, and now independent architecture and engineering.

  1. 2006 – 2014

    GIS Development

    Started as a GIS developer: contributed to a disaster-response WebGIS for a humanitarian relief organization and built ArcGIS plugins synchronizing utility subscriber data with Oracle, working directly with project owners on requirements.

  2. 2012 – 2015

    Web & Field Applications

    WebGIS and Android sales-management tools, an ASP.NET MVC printing-services platform with a B2B panel, and leading an ASP.NET MVC sales-assistance application with tracking, spatial queries and a mobile API.

  3. 2016 – 2017

    WebGIS & Mobile

    Senior WebGIS and Android developer: Django/PostgreSQL WebGIS with offline-map Android apps for city data collection, and ArcEngine vehicle tracking with MapProxy tile caching.

  4. 2017 – 2024

    Technical Leadership

    Senior software developer and team lead for a municipal automation platform of 10+ interconnected subsystems — workflows, WebGIS, reporting, digital files and digital signatures — including the municipal services portal and Building Violation Commission software.

  5. 2017 – 2024

    Backend Architecture

    Led modernization of five related subsystems from .NET Framework to .NET 7, developed five complex subsystems, and optimized SQL Server procedures, triggers and spatial queries with PostgreSQL synchronization.

  6. 2025 – 2026

    Trading Infrastructure

    Architected and delivered a MetaTrader plugin management and licensing platform, built C++ dealing desk and broker plugins, ran BEP-20 and TRON R&D, and integrated AI coding agents into a verified delivery workflow.

  7. Now

    Independent Engineering & Consulting

    Architecture consulting and hands-on engineering for teams building new systems or modernizing existing ones, through ArtaPattern.
