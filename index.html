Where to start

Start From Your Situation

Three common situations, each with a small, scoped way to begin. Every engagement ends with written outputs you can act on, whether or not we continue together.

Building something new?

01 System Design

For a new product or major capability, before serious development starts: clear system boundaries and an implementation direction.

You receive

  • System boundaries and main flows
  • Architecture decisions, written down with their trade-offs
  • An initial implementation direction for the team

Not included Full implementation, UI design and infrastructure provisioning.

Ask about system design →
Working around a difficult legacy system?

02 Architecture Review

For a team with a running system, often on .NET, that is unsure about its structure or where to take it next: what to improve, what to keep, and where to start.

You receive

  • Issues ranked by impact
  • Options for improvement, including what to keep
  • Recommended next steps and a first slice

Not included Executing the full migration or refactoring, which can follow as a separate engagement.

Ask about a review →
Facing backend performance or reliability issues?

03 Backend Reliability Assessment

For slow responses, recurring errors or failing background processing, investigated before committing to a major rewrite.

You receive

  • Findings from the investigation
  • Hypotheses tested, and what they showed
  • Corrective actions in priority order

Not included Ongoing production support or 24/7 on-call.

Ask about an assessment →

Scope, duration and pricing are agreed after a first conversation, once the problem is clear.
